(\code{initialize.jl}, \code{user\_flux.jl}, \code{user\_source.jl} and \code{user\_bc.jl} are one-line

\section{DynSGS as it runs for this case}
\cm{$\Delta_K=\Delta^{elem}_K/(N+1)$} $=0.1\,H_0$. Steps 1--5 are those of the sibling, unchanged:
\cm{rotation once per step}, \mj{two held steps} ($\nu=\nufl$), the \mj{legacy} residual against the
assembled inviscid RHS (no wall-node zeroing on that path), \mj{element normalization} with floors
$r_{loc}(\bar\rho_K,\bar\rho_K\bar c_K,\bar\rho_K\bar c_K^2,\sqrt{\bar\rho_K}\bar c_K)$, \cm{max over the 8 non-$\psi$ slots},
\cm{cap $\Cmax\Delta_K\max(|\bv|+c_f)$}, and
\[
\nuc=\max(0,\min(0.5\,\Delta_Kw_K,\ \Delta_K^2\mathcal R_K)),\quad \nufl=0.03\,\Delta_Kw_K,\quad
\nu_K=\max(\nuc,\nufl),\quad \kappa^E_K=\max(0.0525\,\nuc,\nufl).
\]
The residual sensor itself does \emph{not} see the reference weighting: it acts only in the assembly.

\section{The viscous operator: reference-weighted conserved form}
\code{rhs.jl:1530} sets \code{dsgs\_ref\_weight[] = true} and \code{rhs.jl:1533} \code{dsgs\_split\_energy[] = true}.
\code{user\_primitives.jl} returns, with $\rho_e=\rho_e(z)$ and $\delta q=q-q_e$,
\[
\text{slots }1\text{--}5:\ \ \frac{\delta\rho}{\rho_e},\ \frac{\delta(\rho u)}{\rho_e},\ \frac{\delta(\rho v)}{\rho_e},\
\frac{\delta E_{nth}}{\rho_e},\ \frac{\delta(\rho w)}{\rho_e};\qquad
\text{slots }6\text{--}9:\ \delta B_x,\delta B_y,\delta B_z,\psi;\qquad
\text{slot }10:\ \rho_e;\quad \text{slot }11:\ \frac{\delta E_{th}}{\rho_e},
\]
and \code{\_expansion\_visc!} multiplies the coefficient of slots 1--5 by the weight $\rho_e$ read at the
quadrature point (\code{rhs.jl:2978}):
\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}lll@{}}
\toprule
Equation & Viscous flux $\bF_V$ & \\
\midrule
$\rho$ & $\nu\rho_e\nabla(\delta\rho/\rho_e)$ & \cm{mass diffusion on}\\
$\rho u,\rho v,\rho w$ & $\nu\rho_e\nabla(\delta(\rho u)/\rho_e)$, \dots & \\
$E$ & $\nu\rho_e\nabla(\delta E_{nth}/\rho_e)+\kappa^E\rho_e\nabla(\delta E_{th}/\rho_e)$ & weight on both parts\\
$B_k$, $\psi$ & $\nu\nabla\delta B_k$, $\nu\nabla\psi$ & unweighted\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
All in divergence form (conservative), zero at rest, \mj{no $\tau\cdot\bv$ or resistive-work term}.
Where $q_e$ is negligible against $q$ it reduces to $\nu\nabla q$ on the fluid slots.

\section{Positivity}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
\item \mj{\code{:lpositivity} false}, \mj{no stage limiter}, \mj{no state modification}: nothing clips,
nothing is added. This is the point of the case.
\item What remains: the flux-only floor $p\leftarrow\max(p,10^{-9})$ in \code{user\_flux!} (shared with the
sibling; the state is not changed), the mass diffusion, the $C_{\min}$ background and the
reference-weighted operator, which diffuses the \emph{relative} departure $\rho/\rho_e$ and so cannot carry a
$-10\%$ departure of the dense side of the transition region across the $25\times$ jump in absolute terms
(the mechanism the sibling needed its floor for, according to the deck header).
\end{itemize}
