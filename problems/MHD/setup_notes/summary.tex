\section*{In one paragraph}
Eight of the nine cases stabilize the collocated CG--SEM with the residual-based \code{DSGS\_MHD} viscosity;
\textbf{KH uses Smagorinsky} and no \dsgs{} key. All eight share the element-wise coefficient
$\nu_K=\max\!\big(\Cmin\Delta_Kw_K,\ \min(\Cmax\Delta_Kw_K,\ \CR\Delta_K^2\mathcal R_K)\big)$ with
$\Delta_K=\Delta^{elem}_K/(N+1)$, $w_K=\max(|\bv|+c_f)$, the max of the normalized residual over the eight
non-$\psi$ slots, mass diffusion on, $\Pr=1$, \code{:dsgs\_rel}$=1$, no cutoff, no freezing; they split on the
sensor (\code{legacy} 5, \code{residual} 3), the normalization scope (\code{element} 5, \code{domain} 3), the
operator (a Laplacian of the conserved state or of its departure in 6, the physical form in OT, and a hybrid
in SV), and the hold (2 steps in 6, none in BL, AJ). Positivity is enforced by a \emph{state} modification in
only four cases: the conservative element limiter (ROT, BL, AJ) and a non-conservative case-local floor (FE);
BW, FE, FED and AJ additionally floor $p$ (and AJ $\rho$) inside the flux only. FED is the limiter-free twin
of FE.

\section*{Common to all cases where it applies (solid underline)}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
\item \textbf{General (9/9):} CG spectral elements on LGL nodes with a lumped mass matrix; \code{TOTAL()} variables
with total energy in slot 4 and the slot order $(\rho,\rho u,\rho v,E,\rho w,B_x,B_y,B_z[,\psi])$; ideal-gas MHD
pressure; \code{CarpenterKennedy2N54} at fixed $\Delta t$; \code{:lvisc => true}; filter, KEP, entropy variables,
Richardson correction and AMR off; CPU only.
\item \textbf{GLM (8/8 in 2D):} $c_h=\max(|\bv|+c_f)$ of the initial (AJ: and inflow) data, constant in time; $\psi$ is
damped by a linear source.
\item \textbf{DynSGS (8/8):} \code{DSGS\_MHD}, one coefficient per element recomputed at every RK stage, element
length $\Delta^{elem}_K/(N+1)$, history rotated once per step, max over the eight non-$\psi$ normalized residuals,
cap $\Cmax\Delta_K\max(|\bv|+c_f)$ with $c_f$ bounded by $\sqrt{(\gamma p+|\bB|^2)/\rho}$ (BW: exact $x$-fast speed),
all multipliers $\mu=1$ (mass diffusion on), \code{:dsgs\_gamma} $=\gamma$, $\Pr=1$, \code{:dsgs\_rel} $=$
\code{:dsgs\_local\_rel} $=1$, no cutoff, no stage freezing, no nodal coefficient, no nodal-$\rho$ scaling, no
reference-RHS subtraction.
\item \textbf{Conservative limiter (3/3):} hook, validation, $\gamma$ from \code{:dsgs\_gamma}, floors at $10^{-6}$ of the
ambient $\rho$ and $p$, element pass that conserves $\rho$, $\rho\bv$, $E$ and leaves $\bB$, $\psi$ untouched, DSS of the
increments, non-conservative node floor as fallback.
\end{itemize}

\section*{Findings: code that disagrees with its comments, and one likely failure}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item \textbf{BW, element form (the default): \code{MethodError} expected on the first \dsgs{} call.} Commit
\code{708bd78} added \code{lthermal = \dots} to the 1D call (\code{rhs.jl:1456}) but not to
\code{compute\_dsgs\_viscosity!(::DSGS\_MHD, ::NSD\_1D)} (\code{SGS.jl:1929}); Julia rejects an undeclared keyword.
Found by reading; not executed here (no Julia in this environment). One-line fix: add \code{lthermal::Bool=false}
to the 1D signature.
\item \textbf{SV: conserved coefficients on physical primitives.} The deck sets \code{:dsgs\_conserved => true} but
\code{user\_primitives.jl} returns $(\rho,u,v,T,w,\bB,\psi)$, so the operator is $\nu\nabla u$, $\nu\nabla T$, \dots{} with
no $\rho$ factor, no stress and no work terms --- neither documented form. The file's header describes the physical
form.
\item \textbf{FE, FED:} the comments that \code{"energy"} keeps the $\tau\cdot u$ term active, and that
$\Delta t=2.5\times10^{-3}$, are stale. FED's header claims the sibling's mesh and time step; it refines the mesh
once and uses $\Delta t=5\times10^{-3}$ (FE: $80\times35$, $7.5\times10^{-3}$).
\item \textbf{FE:} the floor reaches stages 2--5 only through \code{calck = true}; the deck does not set
\code{williamson\_condition = false} as the conservative limiter does.
\item \textbf{OT:} the comment that \code{"rank"} is the code-wide norm default is stale (it is \code{"domain"}); the
comments on $\Delta t$ ($5\times10^{-4}$) and on $32^2$ elements giving $128^2$ points predate the current
$\Delta t=2\times10^{-4}$ and the one-level refinement.
\item \textbf{BW:} the header says SSPRK53, the deck uses \code{CarpenterKennedy2N54}; \code{DSGS.md} \S4.7 quotes a
3\% floor, the deck sets $0.06\,(N+1)/N$.
\item \textbf{KH:} the mesh named by the deck (\code{meshes/gmsh\_grids/hexa\_TFI\_32x32\_unitsquare.msh}) is not in
this checkout.
\end{enumerate}
