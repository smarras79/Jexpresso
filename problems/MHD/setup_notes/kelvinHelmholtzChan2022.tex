\noindent\textbf{Summary.} This case uses \textbf{neither DynSGS nor any positivity mechanism}. Its deck
selects \code{:visc\_model => SMAG()} (Smagorinsky); \code{:lpositivity} is left at \code{false}, the
solver carries no stage limiter and the flux has no floor. It is included in this set because it lives
in \code{problems/MHD}; the stabilization it does use is described below so that the comparison with the
eight \code{DSGS\_MHD} cases is complete.

\section{Stabilization actually used: Smagorinsky (not DynSGS)}
\begin{keytable}
\code{:visc\_model} & \code{SMAG()} & \textbf{not} \code{DSGS\_MHD()}; no \code{:dsgs\_*} key is read\\
\code{:lvisc} & \cm{true} & \\
\code{:}$\mu$ & $(0,1,1,1,1,1,1,1,1)$ & no mass diffusion (all \code{DSGS\_MHD} cases have $\mu[1]=1$)\\
Smagorinsky constants & $C_s=0.21$, $\Pr_t=0.7$, $\mathrm{Sc}_t=0.7$, $\mu_{mol}=1.8\times10^{-5}$, $\kappa_{mol}=2.4\times10^{-5}$ & \code{PhysicalConst} (SI air values, used as is in this nondimensional case)\\
Filter length & $\Delta=\Delta^{elem}_{\max}/N$ (\code{mesh.}$\Delta$\code{effective\_l}) & one value for the whole mesh\\
\end{keytable}
Per LGL node (\code{compute\_sgs\_cache!(::SGS\_SMAG)}, \code{SGS.jl:3343}; reader \code{SGS.jl:3590}):
\[
\mu_t=\rho\,C_s^2\Delta^2|S|,\qquad |S|=\sqrt{2S_{ij}S_{ij}},\ \ S_{ij}=\tfrac12(\partial_ju_i+\partial_iu_j),\ i,j\in\{x,y\},
\]
no Richardson factor (\code{:lrichardson => false}). Slot coefficients, with the primitives
$(\rho,u,v,T,w,\bB,\psi)$ of \code{user\_primitives.jl}:
\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}lll@{}}
\toprule
Equation & Flux & Coefficient\\
\midrule
$\rho$ & --- & $\mu[1]=0$\\
$\rho u,\rho v$ & deviatoric stress $\boldsymbol\tau$ of $(u,v)$ & $\mu_{mol}+\mu_t$\\
$E$ & $\kappa\nabla T+\boldsymbol\tau\cdot\bv$ & $\kappa=\kappa_{mol}+\mu_t/(\rho\Pr_t)$\\
$\rho w$, $B_k$, $\psi$ & $\kappa_s\nabla(w,\ B_k,\ \psi)$ & $\kappa_s=\kappa_{mol}+\mu_t/(\rho\,\mathrm{Sc}_t)$\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
No resistive work is added to $E$ on this path. The coefficient is blind to resolution: it is set by the
strain rate, not by a residual.

\section{Positivity}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
\item \mj{\code{:lpositivity} false}; \mj{no stage limiter}; \mj{no state modification}.
\item No flux-only floor (\code{pressure\_mhd} unguarded).
\item No mass diffusion ($\mu[1]=0$).
\end{itemize}

\section{Observations}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item The mesh named in the deck is not shipped in \code{problems/MHD/kelvinHelmholtzChan2022} nor under
\code{meshes/gmsh\_grids/} in this checkout; only \code{KHI\_32x32\_periodic.geo} is.
\item $\mu_{mol}$ and $\kappa_{mol}$ are \code{PhysicalConst}'s SI values for air; in this nondimensional
setup they act as small constant background coefficients.
\end{enumerate}
